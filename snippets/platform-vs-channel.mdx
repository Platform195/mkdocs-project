## Channel, platform, property and product

These four terms sit at different levels of the same hierarchy, and each one
answers a different question. Keeping them separate makes your reporting much
easier to read — and lets you compare like with like.

| Level | Answers the question | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Channel** | What kind of advertising is this? | `Display`, `Native`, `Email`, `Paid Social`, `Metasearch`, `Video` |
| **Platform** | What system serves or buys it? | `Uplift Ads`, `Meta`, `DV360`, `The Trade Desk`, `Criteo` |
| **Property** | Where does it run? | Your website, your app, a named newsletter |
| **Product** | What exactly was sold? | `Homepage Hero`, `Newsletter Sponsorship` |

- **Channel** is the media type — the kind of ad the audience sees. It doesn't
  depend on any vendor, and it's the level where benchmarks make sense: an
  email click-through rate and an on-site display click-through rate aren't
  comparable, so only compare performance within a channel.
- **Platform** is the technology the ads are bought or served through — a
  system and a commercial relationship, not something the audience sees. One
  channel can run across several platforms (Paid Social on Meta, TikTok and
  Pinterest), and one platform can serve several channels (DV360 runs display,
  video and connected TV).
- **Property** is the specific environment the ad appears in. It's the term
  most often mislabelled as a platform — when you own the inventory, your
  website can *feel* like the platform, but it's the place, not the system.
  Don't add properties as platforms or channels; capture them in your
  products instead.
- **Product** is the sellable unit — the thing booked on a line item.

### Worked examples

| What you sold | Channel | Platform | Property | Product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage banner on your website | `Display` | `Uplift Ads` | Your website | `Homepage Hero` |
| Sponsored listing in search results | `Native` | `Uplift Ads` | Your website | `Sponsored Hotel` |
| Promoted Instagram post | `Paid Social` | `Meta` | Instagram | `Social Boost` |
| YouTube pre-roll | `Video` | `DV360` | YouTube | `Video Extension` |

<Tip>
  A quick test: if an advertiser asks "which channels did we run?", the answer
  should be something like "email and on-site display" — not "Uplift Ads and
  Meta". If your channel list reads like a list of vendors or website names,
  it's mixing levels.
</Tip>
