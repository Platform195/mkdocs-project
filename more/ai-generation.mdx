**AI Generate** drafts copy for you in the fields you already fill in — campaign
briefs, line item briefs and creative components. It reads the rest of the brief
for context and writes in the tone of voice set for your advertisers, so you start from a
relevant draft instead of a blank box. You can also translate a creative's copy
into another language in one go.

Nothing the AI writes is saved until you choose a suggestion and click
<Ui>Save changes</Ui>.

---

## Where you'll find it

Look for the <Ui>AI Generate</Ui> button under a text field:

| Where | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign › Brief** | Campaign Objective, Target Audience, Key Message, Destinations / Products to Promote, Offer Details, Key Dates and Success KPIs |
| **Line item › Brief** | Every line item brief field — for example Creative Objective, Headline Suggestions, Body Copy Guidance and Call to Action |
| **Creative › Creative Components** | Copy fields such as headlines and CTAs, plus <Ui>Translate</Ui> for the whole creative |

## Generate copy for a field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click AI Generate">
    Click <Ui>AI Generate</Ui> under the field you want help with. The
    **AI Generated Text** window opens and shows three suggestions after a few
    seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the suggestions">
    If the field already has text, it appears under **Current** so you can
    compare. If the field is empty, the AI writes from scratch using the rest of
    the brief.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine if needed">
    Not quite right? Type an instruction in the box at the bottom — for example
    `make it shorter` or `mention the £60pp deposit` — and click
    <Ui>Refine</Ui>. New suggestions appear under your instruction. Refine as
    many times as you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a suggestion">
    Click <Ui>Apply</Ui> next to the one you want. It replaces the text in the
    field, and you can still edit it by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click <Ui>Save changes</Ui> to keep it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Line item briefs

Fill in the **campaign brief** first. A line item's **Brief** tab shows the
campaign brief at the top, and the AI uses it when it writes the line item
fields — so even an empty field like **Headline Suggestions** gets suggestions
that match the campaign's offer and audience.

## Creative copy

On a creative, <Ui>AI Generate</Ui> sits under each copy field in
**Creative Components**, next to <Ui>+ Add from source</Ui>. It works the same
way as in the brief. Check that the suggestion fits the field's style and length
— for example, a headline marked **Bold capitalised copy** — and use
<Ui>Refine</Ui> to ask for it, such as `All capitals, under 30 characters`.

## Translate a creative

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Translate">
    On the creative, click <Ui>Translate</Ui> in the **Creative Components**
    header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a language">
    Pick a language in **Translate into**, then click <Ui>Translate again</Ui>.
    Every copy component is translated at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the translations">
    Each component shows the original above its translation, so you can check
    them side by side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click <Ui>Apply</Ui> on a single component, tick several and click
    <Ui>Apply Selected</Ui>, or click <Ui>Apply All</Ui>. Then click
    <Ui>Save changes</Ui>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Applying a translation replaces the original copy. If you need both
  languages, select the creative on the line item's **Creatives** tab, click
  <Ui>Duplicate</Ui>, then translate the copy.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Have a native speaker check translations before they go live — especially
  prices, legal wording and whether the formal or informal "you" is used
  consistently.
</Tip>

## Getting better results

- **Fill in the brief first.** The more context in the campaign brief — offer,
  audience, dates — the more specific the suggestions.
- **Be specific when you refine.** `Mention Crete and the 10% saving` works
  better than `make it better`.
- **Fill in the campaign details.** The AI pulls values such as the offer,
  dates, call to action and landing page from the campaign and product using
  macros. Empty fields mean less for it to work with — see
  [Add macros to the system prompt](/client-configuration/ai-configuration#add-macros-to-the-system-prompt).
- **Always review before you save.** AI suggestions can get facts wrong. Check
  prices, dates and offer terms against the brief.

<Note>
  Suggestions follow the tone of voice and language set in
  [AI configuration](/client-configuration/ai-configuration). If they don't
  sound right for your advertisers, ask your ClientCampaignManager to update it.
</Note>
