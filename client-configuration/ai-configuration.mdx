**AI Configuration** controls how Uplift Hub's AI features write for your
advertisers — creative copy, line item and campaign briefs, audience
descriptions and translations. Set the language and tone of voice once, and
every AI-generated suggestion follows it.

The screen has three tabs: **AI Providers**, **Output Defaults** and
**Generation Parameters**.

---

## AI Providers

The **AI Providers** tab lists the AI services connected to your client and
which features each one powers.

<Frame caption="The AI Providers tab — each card lists the features that provider powers.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-ai-providers.png" alt="AI Providers tab showing an OpenAI provider card" />
</Frame>

To add a provider, click <Ui>Add Provider</Ui>. This takes you to
[Connectors](/client-configuration/connectors), where you connect a new
**Gen AI Connector**.

## Set your output defaults

**Output Defaults** apply to everything the AI writes for your advertisers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Output Defaults">
    Select <Ui>Configuration</Ui> in the sidebar, click
    <Ui>AI Configuration</Ui>, then click the <Ui>Output Defaults</Ui> tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a language">
    Pick the **Default Output Language** — for example `English`, `German` or
    `French`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the tone of voice">
    In **Tone of Voice**, describe how copy for your advertisers should sound —
    style, formality, what to lead with and what to avoid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click <Ui>Save Changes</Ui>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Output Defaults — the default language and a tone of voice description.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-ai-output-defaults.png" alt="Output Defaults tab with language dropdown and tone of voice text" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  The more specific your tone of voice, the better the results. Include the
  English variant (British or American), example phrases and calls to action,
  and anything to avoid — such as jargon, exaggerated superlatives or
  competitor mentions.
</Tip>

## Tune generation parameters

**Generation Parameters** lets you fine-tune each AI feature separately. Each
feature has its own collapsible section:

- **Creative Copy Generation**
- **Line Item Briefs**
- **Campaign Briefs**
- **Audience Descriptions**
- **Translation**

<Frame caption="Generation Parameters for Creative Copy Generation.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-ai-generation-parameters.png" alt="Generation Parameters tab showing provider, model, max tokens, temperature and system prompt" />
</Frame>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Provider** | The connected AI provider used for this feature. |
| **Model** | The provider's model. Newer, larger models usually write better but can be slower. |
| **Max Tokens** | Limits how long the response can be. Higher limits allow more detail but take longer to generate. |
| **Temperature** | Controls how creative or predictable the response is, from `0` (most predictable) to `1` (most creative). |
| **System Prompt** | Instructions the AI follows for every response in this feature. |

### Add macros to the system prompt

Macros are placeholders in double curly braces, such as `{{Account.Name}}`.
When the AI runs, Uplift Hub swaps each macro for the real value from the
record you're working on — the advertiser's name, the campaign dates, the
product's ad spec and so on. Macros are what make suggestions specific to each
advertiser and campaign instead of generic.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place your cursor">
    Click in the **System Prompt** where you want the value to appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type two opening braces">
    Type `{{`. A list of available macros opens below the prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a macro">
    Select the macro you want. It's inserted into the prompt in the right
    format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and save">
    Run a test with <Ui>Generate 3 Options</Ui> to check the values come
    through, then click <Ui>Save Changes</Ui>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Typing {{ in the System Prompt opens the macro list.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-ai-macro-picker.png" alt="System prompt with macros and the macro list open below it" />
</Frame>

#### Useful macros

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Advertiser and client">
    - `{{Account.Name}}` — the advertiser's account name
    - `{{Account.CompanyName}}` — the advertiser's company name
    - `{{Client.Name}}` — your company name
    - `{{Client.CurrencySymbol}}` — your default currency symbol, for example `£`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Campaign">
    - `{{Campaign.Name}}`, `{{Campaign.StartDate}}`, `{{Campaign.EndDate}}`
    - `{{Campaign.CampaignBudget}}`
    - `{{Campaign.CommercialMessaging}}` — the offer or key message
    - `{{Campaign.CreativeFocus}}`
    - `{{Campaign.CopyInclusions}}` — anything the copy must include
    - `{{Campaign.CallToActionCopy}}` — the preferred call to action
    - `{{Campaign.LandingPageURL}}`
    - `{{Campaign.Notes}}`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Line item">
    - `{{LineItem.LineItemName}}`, `{{LineItem.StartDate}}`, `{{LineItem.EndDate}}`
    - `{{LineItem.Budget}}`
    - `{{LineItem.Notes}}`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product">
    - `{{Product.Name}}` — the placement, for example `Homepage Hero`
    - `{{Product.Description}}`
    - `{{Product.FormatAdSpec}}` — the ad format and spec
    - `{{Product.Notes}}` — product guidance
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The field being written">
    Use these so the AI knows exactly which field it's filling in and how long
    the answer can be.

    - **Campaign briefs:** `{{ClientCampaignBriefField.FieldName}}`,
      `{{ClientCampaignBriefField.FieldDescription}}`,
      `{{ClientCampaignBriefField.CharacterLimit}}`
    - **Line item briefs:** `{{CreativeBriefField.FieldName}}`,
      `{{CreativeBriefField.FieldDescription}}`,
      `{{CreativeBriefField.CharacterLimit}}`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI settings">
    - `{{AIParameters.ToneOfVoice}}` — the tone of voice from **Output Defaults**
    - `{{AIParameters.DefaultLanguage}}` — the default output language
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For example, a creative copy prompt might read:

```text
You are a performance copywriter for {{Client.Name}}, writing ad copy for the
{{Product.Name}} placement ({{Product.Description}}) on behalf of the
advertiser {{Account.Name}}. Ad spec: {{Product.FormatAdSpec}}. Campaign:
{{Campaign.Name}}, running {{Campaign.StartDate}} to {{Campaign.EndDate}}.
Commercial messaging: {{Campaign.CommercialMessaging}}. Must include:
{{Campaign.CopyInclusions}}. Preferred call to action:
{{Campaign.CallToActionCopy}}. Respect the character limit for the field
exactly. Return the copy only, with no quotes, labels or commentary.
```

<Warning>
  Only use macros that describe the advertiser, campaign, product or field.
  The list also contains internal IDs and system settings — don't add these to
  a prompt, because everything in the prompt is sent to your AI provider.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  A macro is only as good as the data behind it. If a campaign field such as
  **Commercial Messaging** is empty, the AI gets nothing for that macro — so
  fill in the record first for the best results.
</Tip>

### Undo unsaved changes

When you change a feature's settings, a banner shows that the configuration
differs from the last saved version. Click <Ui>Reset</Ui> to go back to the
last saved settings, or <Ui>Default</Ui> to restore the original defaults.

### Test before you save

<Steps>
  <Step title="Adjust the settings">
    Expand a feature and change its model, temperature or system prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a test">
    In **Test Prompt**, click <Ui>Generate 3 Options</Ui>. Uplift Hub sends a
    real request using that feature's current settings and shows three
    suggestions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    When you're happy with the results, click <Ui>Save Changes</Ui> in the top
    right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Test Prompt and the other AI features, each in its own section.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-ai-test-prompt.png" alt="Test Prompt panel with Generate 3 Options button and collapsed feature sections" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  Switching to a different feature resets the test conversation.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use a low temperature (around `0.2`–`0.4`) for briefs and translations, where
  accuracy matters, and a higher one (around `0.7`–`0.8`) for creative copy,
  where you want more variety.
</Tip>
