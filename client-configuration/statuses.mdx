**Statuses** control the stages a campaign or line item moves through, and how
each stage looks in tables, boards and status pills. You can add your own
campaign statuses to match how your team works — for example a
`Contact Initiated` or `PCR Ready` stage. Line item statuses are fixed.

## Built-in and custom statuses

The **Campaign Statuses** table lists every campaign status in the order it
appears across Uplift Hub.

- **Built-in statuses** — such as `Upcoming`, `Live`, `Complete`, `Paused` and
  `Archived` — drive the platform's workflow. You can't edit, move or delete them.
- **Custom statuses** are ones your team has added. They show
  <Ui>↑</Ui> <Ui>↓</Ui> and **⋮** controls at the end of the row.

---

## Add a campaign status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Statuses">
    Select <Ui>Configuration</Ui> in the sidebar, then click the
    <Ui>Statuses</Ui> tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a status">
    Click <Ui>Add Status</Ui> above the **Campaign Statuses** table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **New Status Name**, choose a **New Status Colour** and pick an
    **Icon**. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position it">
    Use the <Ui>↑</Ui> and <Ui>↓</Ui> buttons to move the status to the right
    place in the workflow, then click <Ui>Save changes</Ui> in the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Colours

Choose from `White`, `Yellow`, `Neutral-3`, `Neutral-4`, `Orange`, `Cyan`,
`Teal`, `Critical`, `Blue`, `Red`, `Green` and `Purple`.

<Tip>
  Pick a colour and icon close to the built-in status your new stage sits next
  to — for example, a progress icon for an in-between stage — so the board and
  timeline views stay easy to scan.
</Tip>

---

## Edit or delete a custom status

Click the **⋮** menu at the end of a custom status row:

- <Ui>Edit</Ui> — change the name, colour or icon, then click <Ui>Save</Ui>.
- <Ui>Delete</Ui> — remove the status.

<Warning>
  Move any campaigns out of a status before you delete it, so they don't end up
  without a valid stage.
</Warning>

## Line item statuses

The **Line Item Statuses** table shows the fixed workflow every line item
follows. You can view it here but not change it.

In order, the line item statuses are:

1. `Reserved`
2. `Ready for Production`
3. `In Production`
4. `Ready For Approval`
5. `Approved`
6. `Ready to Go Live`
7. `Live`
8. `Evidence Ready`
9. `Complete`
10. `Paused`
11. `Archived`

See [Go-live workflow](/going-live/workflow) for how line items move to
`Live`.
