**Connectors** link Uplift Hub to the outside services it works with. There
are two kinds:

- **Advertising Connectors** activate line items on third-party ad platforms.
- **Gen AI Connectors** choose the AI providers that power Uplift Hub's
  [AI features](/client-configuration/ai-configuration) for your client.

## Add a connector

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    Select <Ui>Configuration</Ui> in the sidebar, then click the
    <Ui>Connectors</Ui> tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a connector">
    Click <Ui>Add Connector</Ui> in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a service">
    Find the service under **Advertising Connectors** or **Gen AI Connectors** —
    use the search box to narrow the list — and click its tile. Gen AI options
    include **Anthropic Claude**, **Google Gemini** and **OpenAI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise the connection">
    A secure sign-in page opens for that service. Follow the steps to sign in
    or enter your API key, then return to Uplift Hub.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The New Connector dialog.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-connectors-new.png" alt="New Connector dialog listing Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini and OpenAI" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  If **Advertising Connectors** shows `No integrations available`, no ad
  platforms are enabled for your client yet. Contact your Platform 195
  representative to enable one.
</Note>

---

## Connector ownership

Each connection belongs to the user who created it — its **owner**. Only the
owner can manage or re-authenticate the connection, because it uses their
credentials.

---

## Re-authenticate a connector

A warning icon on a connector card means the connection needs attention — for
example, an expired key or revoked access. If you're the owner, click
<Ui>Re-authenticate</Ui> on the card and sign in again.

<Warning>
  Features that rely on a connector stop working until it's re-authenticated.
  For a Gen AI connector, that includes the features listed on its card.
</Warning>

---

## Rename, transfer or delete a connector

Click the **⋮** menu on a connector card:

- <Ui>Edit label</Ui> — change the **Connector Label** shown on the card.
- <Ui>Transfer Ownership</Ui> — hand the connection to another user. Choose
  the new owner in **Transfer to** and click <Ui>Save</Ui>. Ad accounts and
  products are not affected.
- <Ui>Delete</Ui> — remove the connection.

<Frame caption="Transfer Ownership — pick the user who should own the connection.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-connectors-transfer-ownership.png" alt="Transfer ownership dialog with a Transfer to dropdown" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Before someone who owns a connector leaves your team, transfer ownership to
  another user so the connection can still be re-authenticated.
</Tip>
