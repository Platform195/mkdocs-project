**Configuration** is where you manage the settings shared across your whole
client account — the options people pick from when they create accounts,
campaigns, line items and creatives. Changes here apply to everyone on your
account, so make them deliberately.

<Frame caption="The Configuration screen — each tile opens one area of client configuration.">
  <img src="https://cdn-uplifthubs.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/Documentation/client-config-hub.png" alt="Configuration screen showing eight tiles" />
</Frame>

---

## Who can use it

Users with the **ClientCampaignManager** role can open <Ui>Configuration</Ui>
in the left sidebar. Other client roles don't see it. See
[User roles](/setup/roles) for the full permission list.

---

## Opening configuration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Configuration">
    Select <Ui>Configuration</Ui> at the bottom of the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an area">
    Click the tile for the area you want to manage. Use the breadcrumb at the
    top of the page to return to the **Configuration** screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can configure

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Account types" icon="building" href="/client-configuration/account-types">

    The platforms your products are delivered on, such as on-site or social.

    The sales or delivery channels you report against.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Targeting presets" icon="bullseye" href="/client-configuration/targeting-presets">
    Reusable targeting rules for line items and creatives.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI configuration" icon="sparkles" href="/client-configuration/ai-configuration">
    Language, tone of voice and model settings for AI-generated copy and briefs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connectors" icon="plug" href="/client-configuration/connectors">
    Connect ad platforms and AI providers to Uplift Hub.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Configuration is set per client. If you work across more than one client,
  check the client name at the top of the sidebar before you make changes.
</Note>
