A **channel** is the media type — the kind of advertising the audience
experiences, such as `Display`, `Native`, `Email`, `Paid Social`,
`Metasearch` or `Video`. It answers *what kind of ad is this?* and doesn't
depend on which vendor delivers it. The channel appears on a line item's
product details, so you can filter and benchmark performance by channel.

## Your channel list

The **Channels** list works the same way as
[Platforms](/client-configuration/platforms): each channel has a name and an
automatically assigned **ID**.

---

## Add a channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    Select <Ui>Configuration</Ui> in the sidebar, then click the
    <Ui>Channels</Ui> tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new channel">
    Click <Ui>Add Channel</Ui> in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Channel Name**, then click <Ui>Create Channel</Ui>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename or delete a channel

- **Rename** — click the channel's name, update it and save.
- **Delete** — click the **⋮** menu at the end of the row and select <Ui>Delete</Ui>.

<Note>
  If the list is empty, no channels have been set up for your client yet. Add
  the channels your team reports on before you set up products.
</Note>
